# PBI 1208: Record partial payments

- Type: Product Backlog Item
- State: Active
- Area Path: Ledgerline\Payments
- Iteration: Ledgerline\Sprint 5

## Description

As a bookkeeper I want to record a part of the invoice total so customers who pay in instalments are handled correctly.

## Acceptance criteria

- Record payment dialog offers Full amount and Partial amount
- A partly paid invoice stays open with status Partially paid
- The remaining balance is shown on the invoice and in the dialog
- Reconciliation can match a transaction to part of an invoice
- Invoice list gets a Partially paid filter
