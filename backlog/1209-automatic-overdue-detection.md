# PBI 1209: Automatic overdue detection

- Type: Product Backlog Item
- State: New
- Area Path: Ledgerline\Invoices
- Iteration: Ledgerline\Sprint 6

## Description

As a bookkeeper I want open invoices to become Overdue after their due date without checking by hand.

## Acceptance criteria

- Open invoices past their due date become Overdue
- Paid, Credited and Draft invoices never change
- Days overdue never goes below zero
